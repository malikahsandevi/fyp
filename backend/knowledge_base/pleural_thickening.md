Pleural thickening is thickening of the pleural lining, seen as a smooth or irregular soft-tissue band along the chest wall, sometimes with blunting of the costophrenic angle or calcified plaques.

Common causes include prior infection or haemorrhage, previous effusion, and asbestos exposure. Irregular or nodular thickening may need further assessment with CT.
