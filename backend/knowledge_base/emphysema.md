Emphysema is a form of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease with permanent destruction of airspace walls. Chest radiograph signs include hyperinflation, flattened hemidiaphragms, increased retrosternal airspace, and reduced vascular markings, sometimes with bullae.

Radiographs are relatively insensitive for early emphysema, and CT is more accurate. Diagnosis and severity assessment rely on pulmonary function testing and clinical history.
