Pulmonary fibrosis is scarring of lung tissue. Radiographs show reticular (net-like) opacities, often basal and peripheral, with reduced lung volumes; advanced disease may show honeycombing.

High-resolution CT is the preferred modality to characterise the pattern, and causes range from idiopathic disease to connective tissue disease, drugs and occupational exposure.
