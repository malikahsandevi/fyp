A pulmonary mass is a focal lung lesion larger than 3 cm, whereas smaller lesions are termed nodules. A mass appears as a dense, often irregular or lobulated opacity and is suspicious for malignancy until proven otherwise.

A suspected mass on chest radiography warrants further evaluation, typically CT of the chest, and tissue diagnosis may be required.
