A pulmonary nodule is a rounded opacity up to 3 cm in diameter surrounded by lung. Small nodules are easily missed on radiographs, particularly when overlapped by ribs, clavicles or the heart.

Causes include granulomas from prior infection, benign tumours and malignancy. CT is needed to assess size, density and margins, and follow-up depends on these features and on patient risk factors.
