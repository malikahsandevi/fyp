Pneumothorax is air in the pleural space. On an upright chest radiograph a thin visceral pleural line is seen separated from the chest wall, with absent lung markings beyond the line, usually at the apex.

Signs of tension (mediastinal shift away from the affected side, flattened hemidiaphragm) indicate a clinical emergency. Skin folds and bullae can mimic a pneumothorax, so clinical correlation is essential.
