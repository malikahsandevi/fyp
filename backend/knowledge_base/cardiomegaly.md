Cardiomegaly means an enlarged cardiac silhouette. On a frontal PA chest radiograph a cardiothoracic ratio (maximum heart width divided by maximum thoracic width) greater than about 0.5 suggests an enlarged heart.

AP and supine projections magnify the heart, so a borderline ratio on such films should be interpreted with caution. An enlarged silhouette can reflect cardiomyopathy, valvular disease, or pericardial effusion, and echocardiography is needed to establish the cause.
