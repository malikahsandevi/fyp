In chest radiograph datasets the label hernia mostly refers to hiatal hernia, where part of the stomach herniates through the diaphragm into the chest. It typically appears as a retrocardiac soft-tissue opacity, often containing an air-fluid level or gas bubble.

Small hernias are frequently incidental, and further evaluation depends on symptoms; contrast study or CT can confirm the diagnosis.
