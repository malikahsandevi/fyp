Infiltration is a non-specific term for an ill-defined area of increased lung opacity, which may represent airspace or interstitial disease. In the NIH ChestX-ray14 label set it is a broad, low-specificity category.

The differential includes infection, pulmonary oedema, haemorrhage and inflammatory disease, so the finding needs clinical correlation and often follow-up or further imaging to establish a cause.
