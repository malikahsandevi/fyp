Pneumonia is infection of the lung parenchyma. Typical radiographic findings are airspace opacity or consolidation, which may be lobar or patchy, frequently with air bronchograms.

Radiographic changes can lag behind clinical illness, and similar opacities occur with oedema, haemorrhage or atelectasis, so diagnosis depends on symptoms, examination and laboratory results together with imaging.
